1. Static Cat (Verb: "PET!")
   The Visuals: A grumpy, hand-drawn long-haired cat sitting on the notebook lines. The cat is glowing with a jagged neon-yellow highlighter aura (representing static electricity).

The Gameplay: Copying the simplicity of the mosquito game, three sparks of static electricity (stylized as mini lightning bolts) pop up on the cat's fur. A ring contracts around each spark. The player must click the sparks exactly when the rings align.

The Juice: Each perfect click triggers a crackling "ZAP!" sound effect and makes the cat's fur puff up wildly. Clicking all three makes the cat purr and melt into a happy, flat puddle of ink with heart doodles floating up.

2. Window Dash (Verb: "SHUT!")
   The Visuals: A heavy summer rainstorm is blowing through an open, hand-drawn window on the left side of the page. Huge, neon-blue ink drops are flying across the paper toward your pristine sketchbook lines.

The Gameplay: The player's cursor becomes a giant pointing finger. The window frame has a neon-yellow slider handle on it. The player must click and rapidly drag the slider from left to right in one smooth, swift motion to slam the window shut before 3 drops hit the page.

The Juice: Slamming it successfully triggers a loud "SLAM!" text pop-up, screen shake, and a muffled audio filter over the background rain sounds, giving an instant sense of shelter and safety.

3. Flashlight Flicker (Verb: "SMACK!")
   The Visuals: The screen goes almost completely black, except for a weak, dim, flickering cone of light coming from a hand-drawn flashlight barrel at the bottom of the page. The battery indicator is flashing an angry neon-pink.

The Gameplay: Pure rhythmic impact. The player must rapidly and rhythmically left-click anywhere on the screen three times in a row—simulating physically smacking the back of a dying flashlight against their palm to get the batteries to sit right.

The Juice: With each click, a giant comic-book style "WHACK!" hand overlays the screen. On the third click, the flashlight beam explodes into a brilliant, steady, screen-clearing neon-yellow spotlight that reveals a hidden doodle of a smiling ghost who vanishes.

4. Toast Trap (Verb: "CATCH!")
   The Visuals: A retro, sketch-ink toaster sits at the bottom of the page. Two slots are glowing with a hot neon-orange warmth.

The Gameplay: The player hovers their cursor (now a wide-open baseball mitt doodle) right above the toaster. After a random, tense 1-to-3 second delay, two slices of golden toast violently launch into the air. The player must swiftly move the mouse left and right to catch both pieces in mid-air before they fall past the toaster.

The Juice: Catching them rewards the player with a satisfying mechanical "DING!" and a puff of sketchy white steam. If you miss, they land on the paper with a dull thud, immediately turning into burnt, black charcoal scribbles.

5. Soda Explode (Verb: "TAP!")
   The Visuals: A slightly crumpled soda can sits in the center of the notebook. It has been dropped or shaken, and the tab is vibrating violently, surrounded by fizzing neon-pink warning bubbles.

The Gameplay: The side of the can has a designated target zone highlighted in neon-yellow. The player must rapidly click (tap) the side of the can exactly 5 times in 3 seconds to settle the internal pressure before clicking the tab to open it.

The Juice: Every tap makes a metallic "clink" sound and shrinks the danger bubbles. Opening it successfully results in a refreshing, crisp "PSSSHHH" soda sound and a neat little pouring stream. Failing to tap enough causes the can to violently spray neon-pink foam all over the notebook, drowning the interface in ink.


6. Mirage Whack (Theme: Heat Mirage)
   The Visuals: Three adorable, hand-drawn little desert jerboas (rodents) pop their heads out of holes on the notebook page. However, because of the intense heat shimmer, wavy pencil lines blur the screen, and two of the jerboas are translucent "mirages." Only one is solid black ink.

The Gameplay: Just like the mosquito game, contracting rhythm rings appear around all three. The player must click the solid jerboa exactly when the ring aligns. Clicking a translucent mirage results in a poof of empty ink.

The Juice: A correct hit triggers a cartoon "BOOP!" with a shower of neon-yellow sparkles. The jerboa ducking back into its hole safely.

7. Thermometer Tap (Theme: Heat Debt)
   The Visuals: A giant, hand-drawn laboratory thermometer stretches across the page. The liquid mercury inside is a bright, angry neon-orange, rapidly rising toward an explosive "BOILING POINT" line at the top.

The Gameplay: Three neon-blue "Ice Cube" doodles appear along the side of the thermometer. Each ice cube has a shrinking contraction ring around it. The player must tap the ice cubes in sequence as the rings align to drop ice into the meter and force the heat debt back down.

The Juice: Every successful tap plays a refreshing, frozen "CRACKLE" sound effect, and a wave of neon-blue marker ink washes down the thermometer, cooling it off. Failing causes the glass thermometer to shatter into tiny ink shards.

8. Hot Potato Match (Theme: Too Hot to Touch)
   The Visuals: A hand-drawn stick figure's hands are at the bottom of the page. A glowing, sizzling neon-orange charcoal briquette is dropped into their palms. It is literally too hot to hold.

The Gameplay: The stone must be tossed back and forth between the left and right hands to prevent burns. A ring contracts over the empty hand. The player must click the empty hand right as the ring aligns to throw the hot stone over before the current hand starts smoking. Repeat 3 times rapidly.

The Juice: Each successful catch makes a funny "OUCH! HOT!" or cartoon squeak sound effect. If the player holds it too long, a "SIZZLE!" text bubble appears, and the hand turns into a blackened, comical soot cloud.

9. Fan the Flame (Theme: Heat Debt)
   The Visuals: A sleepy doodle character is trying to take a nap on a beach towel, but a massive, aggressive sun doodle in the corner is sweating neon-orange heat waves down on them, filling up a "Heat Stress" bar.

The Gameplay: The player's cursor becomes a giant hand-drawn paper fan. Three rhythmic targets appear in the air right in front of the sleeping character. The player must click the targets in rhythm to wave the fan, blowing the heat waves backward off the page.

The Juice: A perfect rhythmic fan stroke triggers sweeping, cool neon-blue wind lines across the notebook and a refreshing "WOOSH" audio cue. The sun doodle makes a shocked, disappointed face.

10. Shadow Jump (Theme: Heat Mirage / Too Hot to Touch)
   The Visuals: A side-scrolling view of a little ink-blot character running across a scorching hot metal pipeline. The pipeline is glowing neon-orange (too hot to touch). The only safe zones are the moving shadows cast by spinning fan blades overhead.

The Gameplay: The ink-blot character automatically steps forward. As they approach an open, unshaded segment of the superheated pipe, a contracting ring appears on the next shaded spot. The player must click the shadow precisely as the ring aligns to make the character safely leap from one cool shadow to the next.

The Juice: Landing in the shadow plays a relieving "Phew!" sigh. Missing the timing means the character lands on the hot pipe, bounding high into the air while clutching their burning feet with a comical fire-alarm sound effect.
