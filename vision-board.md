Visual Style & Aesthetic Guide
The World (Outer Frame): Cozy, dark, and warm. Dominated by deep blues, purples, and the soft amber glow of a flashlight.

The Notebook (Active Gameplay): Stark white paper with blue lines and red margins. The art inside the notebook looks like loose, sketchy black ballpoint pen doodles with occasional splashes of highlighter yellow or neon blue representing interactive elements.

The "Juice" (Effects): Scratchy pencil-drawn action lines, jittery hand-drawn animations (running at a deliberate, stylized 12 frames per second), and tactile screen shakes.

The 8-Panel Game Feel Storyboard
Panel 1: The Title & Frame (The Setup)
What you see: A top-down view of a cluttered wooden desk in a dark room. Raindrops streak against a windowpane in the background. In the center of the screen is an open, spiral-bound paper notebook lit by the circular beam of a brass flashlight.

The Details: On the desk around the notebook lie half-eaten snacks, a smartphone displaying a "No Connection / Searching for Wi-Fi..." spinning icon, and a couple of crumpled paper balls.

The Game Feel: Cozy, quiet, and slightly nostalgic. The soft hum of a rainstorm plays in the background. The text "MIDNIGHT ANALOG" is scribbled in rough, bold ink across the top of the notebook page. A pulsing button prompts: [ Press Space to Wake Up ].

Panel 2: Transition into the Mind (The Ink Spills)
What you see: The camera smoothly zooms directly into the texture of the notebook page until the wooden desk disappears. The blue lines of the paper become the horizon.

The Details: A hand-drawn, cartoon version of the player’s avatar (a tired-looking kid with messy hair and eye bags) drops from the top of the screen onto the page with a soft splat sound. Ink splatters fly off them like dust.

The Game Feel: Seamless transition. The realistic ambient rain sound fades, replaced by a lo-fi, acoustic guitar track played on a slightly out-of-tune instrument. A large, sketchy countdown timer appears in the corner: 3... 2... 1... GO!

Panel 3: Micro-game 1 – "CONNECT!"
What you see: The page instantly flips. At the center is a shaky, hand-drawn internet router with a blinking red light. A thick, chaotic power cord is unplugged, violently thrashing around the page like an angry snake.

The Goal: You have 4 seconds to plug it in.

The Gameplay: The player uses the mouse cursor (which looks like a giant, hand-drawn pointing finger) to click and drag the plug. As you drag it, the cord resists with physics-based tension, stretching and vibrating.

The Game Feel: High panic. The timer in the top-right is a burning matchstick rapidly turning to ash.

Panel 4: Micro-game 1 – Success!
What you see: The plug snaps into the router socket with a satisfying, loud "CLACK!" sound effect.

The Details: A massive, neon-green wave of "Wi-Fi signals" radiates outward across the notebook page in a sketchy marker texture. The text "CONNECTED!" is stamped in messy red ink across the screen. The avatar in the corner does a quick fist-pump.

The Game Feel: Instant gratification. A triumphant "ding-ding!" chime plays, and the camera rapidly skews to the right as the notebook page aggressively sweeps/flips to the next game.

Panel 5: Micro-game 2 – "SWAT!"
What you see: A close-up of a freckled, hand-drawn arm. Three cartoon mosquitoes with giant, glowing red eyes are hovering over it.

The Goal: Kill the pests before they bite.

The Gameplay: A rhythmic ring expands and contracts around each mosquito (similar to a rhythm game like Osu!). The player must click exactly when the ring matches the mosquito's body.

The Game Feel: Rhythmic and punchy. Missing a click results in a hilarious "buzzing" vibration on the controller/mouse and a small red ink blotch appearing on the arm. Successful hits result in a satisfying "SMACK!" text bubble popping up.

Panel 6: Micro-game 3 – "MELT DEFENSE!"
What you see: A triple-scoop ice cream cone is leaning precariously to the left. Droplets of cream are starting to fall toward a clean, hand-drawn rug at the bottom of the page.

The Goal: Keep the ice cream on the cone.

The Gameplay: The player must frantically swing their mouse left and right across the ice cream to "blow" cold air (visualized as swirling, sketchy blue wind lines) to freeze it back into place and balance the cone.

The Game Feel: Slippery, frantic balancing physics. If you blow too hard to the right, the cone tips dangerously to the left. The player is constantly micro-adjusting.

Panel 7: The "Boss" Game – "FIND THE SIGNAL"
What you see: After completing 10 rapid micro-games, a "BOSS STAGE" banner slams down. The screen shows the player character holding an old analog TV antenna on top of a sketchy roof under a starry sky.

The Goal: Find the perfect angle to catch a signal.

The Gameplay: The player must slowly rotate the mouse to turn the antenna. The screen is covered in heavy, gray pencil-shading (representing TV static). As you get closer to the correct angle, the static clears to reveal a hidden doodle (e.g., a cartoon cat dancing).

The Game Feel: Tense but atmospheric. The audio transitions from loud, annoying white noise static to clear, beautiful retro music as you find the sweet spot.

Panel 8: The Loop Completes (Back to Reality)
What you see: The camera zooms back out of the notebook. We are back at the cozy wooden desk. The smartphone next to the notebook suddenly lights up. The "No Wi-Fi" icon is gone, replaced by a full signal bar and a flurry of notification pop-ups.

The Details: The sun is just starting to peek through the window, turning the deep blues of the room into soft pinks and oranges. The notebook lies open, completely filled with colorful, chaotic drawings of the games you just played.

The Game Feel: Relief, closure, and satisfaction. A summary screen hand-drawn on the final page shows your high score, how many "3 AM Ideas" you successfully brought to life, and a prompt to "Sleep" (Exit) or "Stay Up Another Night" (Endless Mode).
