# Midnight Analog

## Short Description

It's 3AM and the Wi-Fi's down — so every doodle in your notebook becomes a frantic 5-second game. A hand-drawn micro-game rush.

## Description

It's 3 AM at a lakeside cabin. The power's out, the Wi-Fi's down, and you can't sleep — so you scribble strange ideas in a spiral notebook, and each becomes a frantic five-second doodle game. Plug a thrashing router cable, swat mosquitoes, go Super Saiyan, stomp bugs, tune a TV antenna through the static, and more. Survive a night of escalating micro-games and a boss on three hearts, until the morning sun rises. It's all hand-drawn ballpoint on paper.

## Controls

- **Mouse or Touch** — one pointer does everything: tap targets, grab and drag, swipe, and mash. No buttons to learn.
- **Space / Enter** — wake up and start dreaming (and, on the morning screen, stay up for another night).
- Plays anywhere: desktop, tablet, and phone in both portrait and landscape.

## How to Play

1. A one-word instruction flashes across the page — **CONNECT!**, **SWAT!**, **TUNE IT!** — and you have just a few seconds to work out what it means and do it.
2. Nail the micro-game to keep your run alive; fumble one and a heart gets scribbled out. You have three.
3. The pace keeps ramping — the pen jitters faster, the beat pitches up. Clear enough games and a **Boss Stage** slams down.
4. Survive the night and the sun rises on a notebook full of the chaos you just played. Then choose to **Sleep** or **Stay Up Another Night**.
